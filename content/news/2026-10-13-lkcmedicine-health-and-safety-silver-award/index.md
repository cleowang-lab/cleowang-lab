We are delighted to share that **Wang Lab has received the Silver Award at the LKCMedicine Health and Safety Awards 2026**, as announced by the LKCMedicine Health & Safety Committee on 13 October 2026.

The awards recognise laboratories for their efforts to build and uphold a strong safety culture, with the committee evaluating laboratory documentation against its audit criteria.

Our special thanks go to our laboratory Safety Representative, **Dr Chen Hui**, and to every member of the team for their care and commitment to laboratory safety. This recognition reflects a shared effort to make our lab a safe and supportive place to work.

Congratulations to the team! We look forward to continuing to uphold high standards of health and safety in our research.
