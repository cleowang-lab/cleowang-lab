The CWang Lab is recruiting **two PhD students** for the **August 2027 intake**. We welcome motivated candidates who have experience in studying aging and neurodegenerative diseases using interdisciplinary approaches in structural biology, biochemistry, cell biology, and multi-omics.

### Application Timeline

Applications for the August 2027 intake are open from **1 October 2026 to 15 November 2026**. Applicants must meet the official NTU admission requirements and submit their formal application within this period. Please review the [NTU PhD programme and application information](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/graduate-programme/doctor-of-philosophy#programme) before applying.

### Preferred Background

In addition to meeting NTU's official admission requirements, preference will be given to **Master's graduates** with prior training or research experience in one or more of the following areas:

* Electron microscopy, including cryo-EM or cryo-ET
* Biochemistry, molecular biology, or structural biology
* Cell biology, neuroscience, or neurodegeneration
* Protein-lipid interactions or mass spectrometry-based multi-omics

Candidates should be curious, self-motivated, collaborative, and interested in learning across disciplinary boundaries.

### Funding and Support

Each position includes:

* Full scholarship support
* A monthly stipend of **SGD 3,000 + living support**
* Funding for one conference during the PhD, including airfare and accommodation
* Access to advanced research facilities and interdisciplinary mentorship at NTU

### How to Apply

Interested candidates are encouraged to contact Dr. Chuchu Wang before submitting the formal NTU application. Please email **[chuchu.wang@ntu.edu.sg](mailto:chuchu.wang@ntu.edu.sg)** with:

* CV
* Academic transcripts
* A brief description of previous research experience and research interests
* Contact information for 2-3 referees

Please use the email subject line:

`PhD Application - August 2027 - Your Name`
